ONLYUSLABS UPDATE — ENQUIRY FORM & WHATSAPP
===========================================

See ONLYUSLABS-ENQUIRY-WHATSAPP-DEPLOY-GUIDE.txt for the complete instructions.

Summary:
- Classic circular WhatsApp logo button on the public pages.
- Enquiry form now collects country and optional estimated budget.
- Country automatically determines the budget currency (USD, GBP, INR, CAD, AUD, EUR or SEK).
- Enquiries are saved to Supabase and appear in Admin -> Enquiries & CRM.
- After the database save, WhatsApp opens with a prefilled enquiry draft. The visitor must press Send in WhatsApp.
- Admin CRM displays country and estimated budget.

For an existing Supabase project, run ONLYUSLABS-ENQUIRY-BUDGET-MIGRATION.sql before deploying the website update.
